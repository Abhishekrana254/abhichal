Cancellation and refunds

Last updated 3 November 2026.

The ₹49 pass is one payment for 30 days. It does not renew and no UPI AutoPay mandate is created. Members on the older ₹149 monthly plan can press Cancel renewal in Account, or cancel the mandate in their UPI app or with their bank.

There is no refund. The ₹49 stays with us because the lessons open the moment you pay.
