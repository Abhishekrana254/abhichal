Cancellation and refunds

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Cancel anytime

Open Account on the site and press Cancel renewal. The next ₹149 charge does not happen. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for. You can also cancel the UPI AutoPay mandate in your UPI app or with your bank.

No refunds

There is no refund. The ₹149 already taken for the current month stays with us, because the lessons open the moment you pay. Cancel stops future charges only.

Questions

Use the contact page with your Razorpay payment ID.
