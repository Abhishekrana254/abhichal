About Formatly

Formatly shows which AI video formats are getting views on YouTube this week, what a channel could earn from them, and how to make each one: a master prompt for ChatGPT that writes ready prompts for Google Flow, with character and voice locks.

It is made and run by Abhishek Rana in India. Every Monday a script re-runs the scan and updates the top videos. Pro is a one-time ₹49 pass for 30 days, with no auto-renewal. Free access includes the baby-comedy recipe and the weekly numbers.
