What you buy

Formatly Pro is a monthly subscription at ₹149 per month. It gives you access, while the subscription is active, to the full recipes, master prompts, character and voice lock blocks, playbooks and checklists on Formatly. Lessons are shown only to signed-in Pro members and are for your own use. Please do not copy, share or resell them.

Automatic renewal

The first payment of ₹149 is taken when you subscribe. After that ₹149 is charged every month on the same day, through Razorpay, using the UPI AutoPay mandate or card you approve at checkout, until you cancel. Your bank or UPI app may ask you to approve each debit above its limit. If a renewal fails, Razorpay may retry for a few days, and access ends when the month you paid for ends.

Cancel anytime

Open Account on the site and press Cancel renewal. No further charges are made. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for. You can also cancel the mandate in your UPI app or with your bank. Prices may change; we will tell you at least 15 days before a change takes effect and you can cancel before it does.

Refunds

Because the lessons open the moment you pay, we do not refund a month that you have been able to use. We will refund in full if: you were charged and could not get access; you were charged twice; or you were charged a renewal after you cancelled. Report it within 7 days of the charge using the contact page, with your Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days.

Accounts and your recovery key

There is no password. You get a recovery key when you subscribe. Keep it safe. If you lose it, you can sign in again with the email you paid with and the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt. We may suspend an account that shares access publicly or resells the content.
