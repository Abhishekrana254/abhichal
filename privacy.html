Privacy

Formatly is run by Abhishek Rana, India. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your Google account email, your name, and a Google account ID. We use these only to recognise your Formatly account. We never see your Google password.
  • When you subscribe: your email, an optional name, the phone number Razorpay shares with us, your Razorpay subscription and payment IDs, and your subscription status and dates.
  • When you write to us: your email, your message and a payment ID if you give one.
  • When you browse (everyone): anonymous counts such as page views, which niche pages are opened, whether the app was installed, and which site sent you. This uses a random ID stored in your own browser, not a cookie, and does not store your IP address. If your browser sends Do Not Track, we send nothing.

What we never see

Your card number, bank details or UPI PIN. Razorpay handles all of that on its own pages.

Why

To give you access, renew and cancel your subscription, answer your messages, prevent fraud and sharing, and understand how many people use the site so we can improve it.

Who has it

Razorpay (payments), Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (database) process data for us. We do not sell your data and do not show ads from it. We share it only where the law requires.

How long

Account records are kept while your subscription is active and for the period needed for tax and payment disputes after it ends. Anonymous counts are kept as totals. Ask us to delete your account and we will, except for records we must keep by law.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, clear the site's storage in your browser, or use Do Not Track. To access, correct or delete your data, use the contact page from the email you paid with.
